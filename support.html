A public support contact must be confirmed before launch.

Can I use my existing account?

Yes. Sign in with your existing Prime Breeder account to access your records. A web account uses the same identity as the mobile application.

Does creating an account start a Trial?

New registration is unavailable during this testing release. The planned 14-day Trial requires an explicit choice when available.

Is Community included in Free?

Yes. Forum, Rooms, Friends and Direct Messages remain available with Free Community.

Can I buy Premium here?

Web checkout is not enabled. Monthly and yearly prices will be published when approved. This preview does not collect payment or card details.

Is Smart Scan ready for everyone?

Smart Scan remains in final-stage testing. It is not generally available. Availability and allowances are controlled by the account, and every suggested pedigree must be reviewed before Save.

Are all tools identical on web and Android?

They use the existing records and business logic. Some browser workflows are still being verified; this is a testing release and commercial activation remains pending.
