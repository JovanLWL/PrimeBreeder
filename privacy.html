Privacy & data handling

Your account and records

Prime Breeder uses the existing Firebase account and services for sign-in, saved records and media. Your browser opens the records available to your signed-in account.

Sharing and community

Information you choose to post in Community or share with another owner follows those features’ existing visibility controls. Review the audience before posting or sharing.

Smart Scan

Smart Scan is in final-stage testing. When available and initiated, it uses Google Cloud Vision to process the pedigree image. It must not be described as a wholly local reader.

Backups and exports

Use the existing Backup & privacy controls and supported PDF or Excel exports in the application. Availability depends on the tool and account.

Publication status

Prime Breeder is currently operated by an individual. This is a product data-handling summary, not a replacement for an approved Privacy Policy. The operator’s contact details, retention periods, legal bases and rights-request procedure must be confirmed before public publication.

Planned Community moderation

Automatic checks of Community text, including private messages and rooms, are planned but are not active in this release. The planned moderation tools do not allow owners or moderators to browse private conversations. Before activation, the processing and review procedure will be documented in the Privacy Policy and Terms.
