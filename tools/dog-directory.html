Explore Prime Dog Directory

What a public profile shows

See basic details such as breed, sex and coat colour. A photo, pedigree, genetic results, selected health results and show titles or results appear only when the owner has chosen to share them. Private kennel records stay private.

Choose what to share

Go to Dog Profile → Dog visibility. Dogs are Private by default and stay out of Directory searches. Choose Public to publish the allowed profile details. You can choose whether to include the pedigree, Genetics, Health and show information.

Plan a mating with your own dog

Open a public dog with a shared pedigree, choose Trial mating, then select an eligible dog from My Dogs. The existing analysis shows COI, AVK, common ancestors and pedigree completeness. Predictions need suitable genetic evidence. Results depend on pedigree depth, matched ancestors and the evidence the owners shared. The calculation leaves breeding, pregnancy and litter records untouched.
