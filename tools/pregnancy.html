Live Whelping

Track every moment of whelping, from the first event to the last puppy.

From breeding to birth

Open an eligible pregnancy linked to an existing breeding attempt and select Start live whelping. This starts the session and timer and creates or reuses one linked litter. Pregnancy and earlier mating and progesterone records remain in the history. If a session is active, Resume live whelping opens the same session. Home can also show a shortcut.

Record a birth or an event

Select Puppy born and choose the puppy’s sex. The session records the birth time, order and link to the litter. You can add birth weight, collar colour, coat colour or markings, placenta information and notes where available. The chronological journal also records pushing, water breaking, placenta passing, calls to the vet and notes. Contextual reminders are informational.

Follow the timers

Before the first birth, the timer shows time since whelping started. After a birth, it shows time since the last puppy. If the first puppy is born at 22:10, the timer shows 25 minutes at 22:35. If the next puppy is born at 22:42, the recorded interval is 32 minutes and the timer starts again from that birth.

Finish and continue in Litters

After recording at least one birth, select Finish whelping when delivery is complete. This closes the session, preserves the timeline and updates the linked litter. My Dogs shows the progression from Pregnant to Whelping in progress to Litter born. The same puppy records continue in Litter Details and Puppies for growth charts, weights, vaccinations, health protocols, photos, buyer assignment and follow-up.

Sync on your devices

Authorized devices signed into the same account can receive session updates. Where offline entry is supported, entries stay pending locally until confirmed after reconnection. Offline behaviour can vary by platform and browser.
